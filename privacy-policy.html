1. Scope and Who Handles Data

This Policy describes data that may be processed when you visit Alaada websites or use Orbit AI, Alaada Sheets, Alaada Accounts, Web Analyser, optional workspaces, reviews, support channels and other features as actually deployed. Alaada currently operates as a developing project/brand; references to a registered company, merchant of record or designated privacy officer should not be inferred from the brand name.

For privacy and data requests, contact arzodas2024@gmail.com. If a paid or organisation product is provided through another identified legal entity, an applicable notice or contract should identify that party and its responsibilities.

2. Categories of Information

Account and identity data

  • Names, email addresses, account or provider IDs, profile images where supplied, login links or authentication metadata, organisation and workspace identifiers.
  • Invitations, memberships, roles, access permissions, preferences and relevant sign-in events, where supported.

Technical and usage data

  • IP addresses, approximate network-based locations, device and browser information, cookies and identifiers, diagnostic logs, timestamps, URLs visited and feature interaction events.
  • Security signals, API usage, error information, service limits, support communications and troubleshooting details.

Content you choose to submit

  • Prompts, AI conversations, voice recordings or transcripts where enabled, generated outputs, uploaded files, images and spreadsheets.
  • Financial and operational records, invoices, customers, suppliers, employees, inventory and other data supplied to accounting or business features.
  • Contact enquiries, feedback, reviews, attached files and URLs submitted for website analysis.

Payment-related information (where paid checkout is enabled)

Plan selection, invoice or subscription identifiers, payment status, receipt details and transaction metadata may be returned by a processor. A third-party payment service ordinarily collects card details directly; the exact provider and its policy will be identified at checkout. A planned gateway is not an active data recipient until it is connected.

3. Why Information Is Processed

  • Create and manage requested accounts, sign-in sessions, memberships and product access.
  • Provide AI responses, processing of files, spreadsheet calculations, accounting functions, website analysis and other user-directed operations.
  • Store, synchronise, share, convert or export content according to available functionality and the permissions you grant.
  • Answer support requests, investigate incidents, diagnose errors, enforce usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • Provide billing, invoices or subscription services if a checkout is activated.
  • Measure usage and improve reliability and design, subject to available controls and applicable law.
  • Meet legal obligations and respond to valid requests.

Where law requires a specific lawful ground, consent, notice or other condition, processing must be limited to the relevant authorised purpose. This Policy is an explanation; it is not a substitute for a required separate consent notice or consent-management control.

4. How Alaada Products Handle Data

Orbit AI

AI requests may include messages, chat history, selected memory or context, uploaded documents, images, selected web-search results and tool arguments. Some processing may occur on third-party model or speech services. Conversation and file retention can depend on the deployment, provider and storage configuration. Do not assume that every session or upload is permanently stored, deleted immediately or inaccessible to service providers.

Alaada Sheets

Spreadsheet content, formulas, import/export files, sharing metadata and version information may be processed by the relevant browser, application server or configured database. Collaboration and per-user permission controls depend on the specific version deployed; exports should be checked and saved independently.

Alaada Accounts and business tools

Records may include account ledgers, financial statements, vouchers, tax fields, bank and payment information, customers, suppliers, staff and documents. These may contain information about people other than the account holder. Business administrators are responsible for lawful collection, notices, access restrictions and record accuracy. Sensitive business data should not be placed into public demos.

Web Analyser

Submitted website URLs and diagnostic results may be processed by the service, which may contact the nominated website and expose the analyser’s IP address or request metadata to that website or its hosting provider. Avoid submitting private URLs, secret-bearing query strings or internal network addresses.

HR and other experimental modules

A standalone HR prototype may run locally in the browser and optionally use browser storage or file imports/exports; it is not automatically a secure, server-backed employee database. Do not use an unaudited prototype for real salaries, identity documents, medical information or confidential employment records.

5. Sign-In, Account Providers and Cross-Product Access

Different Alaada products may rely on Appwrite, Supabase, Google OAuth or other account systems and may not yet share a single account record or authentication session. Providers may receive identifiers and authentication events according to their own notices. We may also receive account IDs and basic profile information necessary to sign users in.

Organisation invitations, entitlements and shared workspaces can use different backends or be limited in an early version. A visible “signed in” badge alone should not be interpreted as proof of current server authorisation. Access and deletion requests may require checking more than one service.

6. AI, Voice, Model Providers and Tool Processing

To deliver an AI request, prompts and relevant content may be sent to external model, speech, search, image or file-processing providers. The data shared depends on the tool you invoke and the context needed for it. Providers may apply their own retention, abuse-monitoring and processing terms; Alaada does not promise that every provider applies identical terms or that all AI inputs are categorically excluded from model training.

Do not enter passwords, banking credentials, government identifiers, medical records or confidential client data unless the particular product is appropriately configured and you have permission to process that information. AI content may be inaccurate and should be reviewed before publication or consequential use.

Where an action executes code, accesses a connected system or sends content to a third party, that action may transfer more data than a plain conversation. Check the destination, permissions and preview before confirming.

7. Who May Receive Information

Depending on a user’s product and its configuration, necessary processors and recipients can include cloud hosting and delivery networks, Appwrite or Supabase identity/database/storage services, Google sign-in and analytics services, AI model or speech services, email or form handlers, payment processors once activated, and external services deliberately connected by the user.

  • People you intentionally invite into a workspace or share content with may see that content within their granted scope.
  • Technical providers may receive data required to perform requested tasks, storage, delivery, billing or security functions.
  • We may disclose information in response to valid legal requirements, or to investigate security incidents and protect lawful rights.
  • We do not sell personal information to advertising data brokers as part of the documented Alaada service model; advertising or analytics providers, where present, can have their own data practices described in their notices.

The precise provider set can change by deployment. No future integration, including a proposed payment provider, should be assumed active unless shown in the service or checkout.

8. Website Forms, Email and Reviews

The current Contact page prepares an email message to arzodas2024@gmail.com through the visitor’s email application; opening an email draft does not itself submit information to an Alaada server. If you send the email, your email provider and our inbox provider will process the message.

Some review or feedback forms may use an external form handler (for example, Formspree) when enabled. In that case, the data you submit may be received and stored by the form service before Alaada accesses it. Public reviews may display details you decide to publish. 9. Cookies, Local Storage and Analytics

Websites and applications may use cookies, session storage, browser local storage and similar mechanisms to hold sign-in state, app preferences, local drafts, feature settings and technical identifiers. Some pages may run Google Analytics, and infrastructure services may maintain technical or security logs.

Optional analytics or advertising technologies, where present, may have separate consent requirements. Browser settings can restrict storage and tracking, although disabling essential authentication storage can prevent a service from working. Do not assume that all local browser data is included in an account export or server-side deletion.

10. Storage, Backups and Hosting Limitations

Alaada products may run on hosting services such as Render and may use Appwrite, Supabase Storage, PostgreSQL or other storage when configured. Some development or free-hosting environments use temporary local file storage that can disappear on restart. Other deployments may persist files in a database or object store. The presence of a storage connector in code does not prove that every product or upload uses it.

Backup, file-sharing controls and recovery differ by service. We cannot guarantee that a file is backed up, recoverable or deleted from all secondary copies on a particular schedule. Keep independent copies of important documents and financial records.

11. Retention and Deletion

Retention depends on the product, what you submit, active sessions, account history, billing and tax requirements, security logs, provider limits and backups. Some local browser files remain until the user clears local storage; some server uploads may expire or disappear on hosting restart; other data may persist until deleted or an applicable retention period ends.

We have not published one verified uniform retention period covering every Alaada product or provider. Where a specific retention period is needed by law or product contract, it should be disclosed at collection before reliance on that feature. A deletion request may require identity verification and may be subject to necessary legal records, backups or external processor procedures.

You may request available export, correction, access, account closure or deletion by contacting arzodas2024@gmail.com. We will assess the request against the product’s actual storage and applicable legal requirements rather than claiming immediate deletion from all systems.

The February 2026 amendments dealing with unlawful synthetically generated information may apply to activities or services that fall within the relevant intermediary provisions.

This description is informational and does not state that all DPDP obligations are already operative as of 14 October 2026, that Alaada has been designated as a regulated intermediary, or that every required compliance mechanism has been completed.

The notified DPDP framework has staggered effective dates after November 2025, including further provisions expected in November 2026 and May 2027. The precise duties depend on the provision, service and its role.
