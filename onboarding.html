Set up your profile. Alaada creates a private personal workspace for every account. You can join an organisation workspace from an invitation at any time.

Checking your Appwrite account and private workspace…
Every Alaada identity keeps its private personal workspace and subscription. Enterprise access is a separate membership: ask your organisation administrator to invite this same email address. Switching account type does not create a seat or expose your personal data.
Payment and paid-plan checkout are handled through Dodo Payments. The live checkout and verified billing webhook are not connected yet, so no charge or paid entitlement can be activated here.
