Alaada OS

Alaada OS isn’t a traditional operating system — it’s a vision of how technology should work together. It represents the core of Alaada’s ecosystem, bringing innovation, design, and intelligence into one connected space.

Within Alaada OS live several unique projects, each built to explore the boundaries of technology:

🧠 Aurora Programming Language

A creative, human-friendly language made to simplify logic and enhance expression.

🧮 Alaada Spreadsheet

A modern, Python-based spreadsheet with formula support and built-in AI.

👁️ Face Detector

Real-time AI that can identify and respond to human faces.

✋ Finger Counter

Vision-powered system for gesture recognition and robotic control.

🎮 Stone–Paper–Scissors AI

A fun, learning-based experiment in pattern recognition and machine learning.

Alaada OS is the start of something bigger — where imagination becomes function.
