What dum means

Dum is slow cooking in a sealed pot. It is why the rice takes on the flavour of the meat, and why the lid stays shut until the pot is ready.

  1. 1

    Marinate

    The meat rests in curd, ginger, garlic and spices.

  2. 2

    Layer

    Meat and part-cooked rice go into the pot in layers, with fried onion and mint.

  3. 3

    Seal

    The lid is closed with dough so no steam escapes.

  4. 4

    Dum

    Low heat, lid unopened, until the rice finishes cooking in the steam.
