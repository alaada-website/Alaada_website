One year ago, on 3 November 2025, Alaada began with an idea: to create something different.

Alaada means “different” in Bengali — a belief that technology should make possibilities accessible. From Orbit AI to Sheets, Accounts and Web Analyser, every product is part of one vision: making useful technology work together.

Every experiment taught us something. We are still learning, and still building.

Today we celebrate the courage to begin, and the possibilities that lie ahead.

This is not the finish line. It is the foundation for everything still to come.

To everyone who explored Alaada, used our products, shared feedback, supported our journey or simply believed in the idea — thank you. You are part of this first chapter.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Alaada?+

Alaada is a technology platform building connected AI, business, and productivity tools. Its current products include Orbit AI, Alaada Accounts, Web Analyser, and Alaada Sheets.

Is Alaada free to use?+

Alaada products are designed to work in the browser. Selected tools offer free access, including Web Analyser without sign-in, while advanced capabilities may vary by product and plan.

What is Alaada Accounts?+

Alaada Accounts is an AI-assisted accounting and business operations platform for managing ledgers, vouchers, reports, sales, purchases, inventory, GST, CRM, automation and imports in one connected workspace.

What does Orbit AI do?+

Orbit AI is a deep-reasoning AI assistant that thinks step-by-step before answering. It supports live web search, file analysis, decision breakdowns, and voice output — all in one chat interface.

What does the Alaada Web Analyser check?+

The Web Analyser audits any website URL for performance score, SEO health, accessibility compliance, and speed metrics like LCP and First Paint. It shows a prioritised fix list so you know what to improve first.

Where is Alaada based?+

Alaada is based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India, and builds its products for both Indian and global users.
